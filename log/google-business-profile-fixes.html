Five fixes for your Google Business Profile this week

For a restaurant, shop or repair business, the Google Business Profile is often the first thing people see. Five small fixes make it complete, current and easy to trust.

October 20, 20264 min readNavy Night Creative

When someone searches for a business like yours, the profile on Google Search and Maps usually shows up before your website does. It answers the first questions people have: are you open, where are you, what do others say. Here are five fixes you can make in an afternoon.

1. Choose the most specific primary category

The primary category tells Google what you are. Pick the most specific one that fits, such as "Turkish restaurant" rather than "Restaurant", then add secondary categories for anything else you offer.

2. Make your hours match reality

Wrong hours cost visits and invite bad reviews. Update regular hours, set special hours for holidays ahead of time, and check them again after any change in staffing or season.

3. Add real photos, and keep adding them

Show the storefront as people see it from the street, the inside, the team and your best products or dishes. Replace photos that no longer match what customers will find when they arrive.

4. Reply to every review

Thank people for good reviews by name and mention something specific. For critical ones, stay calm, keep it short and offer to continue the conversation directly. Future customers read the replies as much as the reviews.

5. Fill in services and post updates

List your main products or services with a one-line description each. Use posts for offers, events and news so the profile looks looked-after rather than abandoned.

None of these takes more than an hour. Done together, they turn a bare listing into a profile that answers questions before people have to ask.
