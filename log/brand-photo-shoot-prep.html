How to prepare for a brand photo shoot

Good shoots are decided before the camera comes out. A little preparation means better pictures, a shorter day and photos you will still be using a year from now.

October 6, 20264 min readNavy Night Creative

A photo shoot is an investment, and most of its value is set in the days before it. Here is how we prepare with clients so the day runs smoothly and every picture has a job.

Start from where the photos will live

List the places the images will be used: website header, menu or catalog, social posts, Stories, ads. Each has its own shape, so the shot list should include both horizontal and vertical versions of the important pictures.

Write a shot list

Turn that list into specific shots: the storefront, the hero product from three angles, the team at work, a detail of the materials. A shot list keeps the day focused and makes sure nothing important is forgotten.

Prepare the space and the products

  • Clean and declutter every area that will be in frame.
  • Choose the best-looking items and keep spares ready.
  • For restaurants, shoot before service when the light and the kitchen are calm.

Brief the people

Decide who will appear and share simple wardrobe guidance: solid colors, no large logos, clothes that fit the brand. Collect permission from anyone who will be recognizable in the published photos.

After the shoot

Select the strongest frames, edit them to one consistent look and organize the archive with clear names and folders. A well-kept library means the team can find the right photo in seconds, long after the shoot day.
