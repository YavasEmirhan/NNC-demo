A painter’s color notebook, on your business.

Sanzo Wada (1883–1967) was a Japanese painter and costume designer. In 1933–1934 he published Haishoku Soukan, a six-volume study that grouped colors not one by one but in sets of two, three and four; today it is printed as A Dictionary of Color Combinations. His costumes for Gate of Hell won the Academy Award for Best Costume Design (Color) in 1955. Inspired by his combinations, we put them to work on the everyday pieces of a business, from a business card to a storefront sign.
